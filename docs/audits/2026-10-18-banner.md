# Banner art audit (2026-10-18)

Scope: the 432x144 banner (`gen/gen_banner.c`, exact piece at columns 136..295, scene continued by a wide pass over x = -136..295), downloaded at 4x (1728x576). Report only, no generator code changed.

## Method

- **Sample:** 2200 banners from the WASM build (`web/gen.wasm` via `web/seedlab.js`, i.e. what collectors get): 2000 pseudo-random u32 seeds (xorshift from 0x12345678) plus seeds 1..200. Plus a targeted set of 20000 seeds (1000000+) with twin moons, double sun, comet, birds, glitch, oasis or volcano for the rare objects.
- **Harnesses** (scratch, not in the repo; they link the unchanged `gen/*.c`):
  1. raw wide pass before the paste, to compare the piece with what the wide pass drew under it;
  2. per-phase snapshots of the 8 columns on each side of both seams plus every ridge layer's `hgt[]` (via the `gen_phase(P_RIDGE)` hook), to attribute each seam mismatch to the phase that caused it and to measure how far the side ridges leave the piece's height range;
  3. an instrumented copy of the generator that logs every object pixel drawn outside 0..159 (normal pass) or on the sides (wide pass), per object: "cut" = the piece wanted to draw past its edge and the wide pass did not draw the same pixel (object sliced at the seam); "ghost" = the wide pass drew that object on a side where the piece has none.
- **Image scans:** shade histograms sides vs centre, 8x8-block structure, isolated-star density, seam column change.
- **Viewing:** contact sheets for every terrain, ground, sky, time, weather and extra (50 sheets, 6 banners each, at 2x), random sheets, zooms of both seams at 4..8x, a few full 4x downloads.

## Centre identity check: PASS

- Banner columns 136..295 == `lab.draw(seed).px` on **2216 of 2216** seeds (WASM), traits identical too.
- Same banner after other banners/draws in between (state leaks from KEEP slots / `rec_hgt`): 300 of 300 identical.
- WASM banner == native `gen_host banner`: 100 of 100.
- Dither phase: `BANNER_X0 = -136` is a multiple of 8 and `x & 7` works on negative x, so the Bayer pattern lines up across the paste. No dither seam found.
- Continuous by construction, no seam found: sky gradient, sun/moon glow, aurora (KEEP 2..5), milky way band geometry (KEEP 0..1), rain/snow cloud deck (KEEP 6), water ripple bands (KEEP 7), fog (KEEP 8), desert sand texture, valley mist, horizon line.

## Defects, ranked

Shares are of the 2200 random banners unless stated. Code lines are in the current tree (HEAD 510c786).

### Critical

**C1. Side ridges climb far above the piece and hit the ceiling: the banner reads as a bowl with the piece sunk in the middle.**
- Far ridge (layer 0): sides rise on average **15.7 px** above the piece's highest point of that ridge, **66%** of banners more than 10 px. **52%** (1154/2200) have some layer more than 20 px above its own range in the piece (worst 68 px). **58%** have a side ridge flattened against the y = 8 clamp (a ruler-straight ridge top).
- Cause: `gen_ridges.c:extend_side` aims the walk at `e[256] = base*8 - rnd(amp*2, amp*6)` (line 65) with displacement `d = amp*4` (line 66) using the full `amp`. The piece's ridge was compressed by the sun/top-edge clearance (`kk`, lines 180-188, skipped when WIDE), so the far ridge in the piece is often a low flat band while the sides use the uncompressed amplitude; for the far layer the target lies above the top edge, and line 76 clamps it at y = 8.
- Seeds: 216055695, 54, 1170147724, 2612710239, 137, 2274908837, 1210119364 (a good banner otherwise, left far ridge to the ceiling). Sheets: `img/banner/03-ridge-overshoot-islands.png`, `01-random.png`.
- Fix: record per layer the piece's `kk`, `minH`, `maxH` in the normal pass. In `extend_side` use `amp * kk / 256` (and the fjord's 5/4), draw the target from inside the piece's own range (`[minH, base]`), and clamp side heights to `max(8, minH - ~4 px)` instead of 8. A soft limit (compress above minH) looks better than a hard clamp.

**C2. Snow becomes tall striped slabs on the sides.**
- **78%** of snowcap banners (405/521, about 18% of the collection) have a snowy layer rising more than 8 px above its top in the piece. The snow line `snowY`/`snowAbs` comes from the piece (lines 200-203), so all of the risen side ridge is snow-coloured. Each column is lit or unlit (`lit ? 0 : 35`, line 225; `lit` from a noisy slope, line 216), so the tall snow turns into **vertical bars**, cut off at the bottom by the straight `snowAbs + jit` line. The worst-looking defect in the sample, and the most common thing in the worst-10 sheet.
- Seeds: 4128058637, 528240171, 1643073169, 1505561488, 2523867658, 344475207, 2284287036, 358294691. Sheets: `02-snow-bars.png`, `02b-snow-bars-zoom4x.png`, `10-worst10.png`.
- Fix: C1 removes most of it. Also cap snow depth to a few px below each column's ridge top (as in the piece), and on the sides take `lit` from a wider-smoothed `hs` (or hysteresis) so it can't flip column by column.

### High

**H1. Clouds sliced at the seams.**
- **34%** of all banners have a piece cloud cut by a seam (more than 3 px): clouds 66%, rain 59%, storm 53%, snowfall 42%, fog 26%, clear 11%. The cut is a hard vertical edge exactly at x = 136 or 295 and is obvious at 4x. A half-cloud often starts on the side right at the seam.
- Cause: `gen_sky.c:draw_clouds` in the wide pass scales `nc` (line 190) and draws `cx = rnd(GX0, GX1)` (line 192), so it makes a completely different set of clouds. The piece's clouds are not continued, and side clouds that cross into the centre are covered by the paste.
- Seeds: 956122723, 2710203498, 3742576563, 1210119364, 2176035992, 2516957623, 2032861259. Sheets: `07b-cloud-cuts-zoom4x.png`, `07-clouds.png`.
- Fix: in the wide pass, first draw the piece's own `nc` clouds with exactly the same RNG calls (same ranges, `cx` in 0..160, no scaling) so they continue past the edge. Then add side clouds from a side stream (`rseed(gen_seed_now ^ K)`, save/restore `rs` like `extend_side`), with centres far enough out (`cx + w/2 + 12 < 0` or `cx - w/2 - 12 >= 160`) that the paste never cuts them.

**H2. Islands turn into long coastlines or mountains on the sides.**
- 662 of 1059 island layers (l > 0) continue as land on a side. `extend_side` sends only an edge that is already open water (SENT) to open water (line 61). An island touching the picture edge becomes a 136 px shore, and with C1 it can rise into a mountain (seed 137: an island layer at y = 90 in the piece climbs to y = 32 on the left).
- Seeds: 54, 137, 25, 28, 4, 3907270133, 3648333025. Sheets: `04-islands-coastline.png`, `03-ridge-overshoot-islands.png`.
- Fix: for `R_ISLANDS` layers l > 0, taper the walk down to the horizon within roughly one island width, then SENT. Optionally scatter 0..2 new small islands per side from the side stream using the island formula (lines 165-176).

**H3. Mesas and dunes lose their character on the sides.**
- Mesas: the side walk is generic midpoint displacement plus one [1 2 1] smoothing (lines 72-74). No plateau quantisation (`smooth256(lv-20, lv+5)` + `winmax`, lines 139-141), so flat-topped buttes become sloping hills. The piece's plateaus end in cliffs and the scenery beyond is a different landform. This is the "banner mesa seam" already on the to-do list.
- Dunes: the sawtooth wave (lines 144-149) is not continued. The sides are soft rolling humps: near-flat step share 0.81 on the sides vs 0.60 in the piece, side height range 26.9 px vs 12.6 px.
- Seeds: 3742576563, 4035130921, 3613565707, 2926455590 (mesas); 1244043175, 2516957623, 3363217481 (dunes). Sheets: `05-mesas.png`, `19-mesa-4x.png`, `06-dunes.png`.
- Fix: run the side walk in profile space (0..256, normalised with the piece's `lo/hi`) and pass it through the same terrain transform as the piece. Mesas: KEEP `lv` and apply the same plateau + winmax. Dunes: KEEP `lam`, `off` and continue `(x + off) % lam` for negative x too. Peaks: the same `p` curve. Then join to the exact edge height by fading the offset over 8..16 px.

### Medium

**M1. Wide-pass random stream diverges, so some objects are cut or ghosted at the seams.**
- The wide pass uses more random numbers before `draw_light`: stars (`n` scaled and `rnd(GX0, GX1-1)`, `gen_sky.c` lines 91-94), milky way `rnext()` per pixel over 432 columns (line 69), `mdisp` skipped in `draw_layer`, extra clouds. Everything drawn after that point gets different values: `side` (line 109), comet, twin moon, palms, birds, lightning. Objects drawn before any divergence (double sun, dusk comets) continue correctly (0 cuts).
- Measured on the targeted 20000-seed set:
  - twin moons: 1.4% cut, 0.8% ghost. Seed 1012471 shows **three moons**; 1002098 has the small moon halved at the seam.
  - comet: 5.1% cut, 5.8% ghost. Night comets only (dusk 0%). Seed 531160323 has the tail ending at the right seam.
  - oasis palms: 5.2% cut, 5.7% ghost (fronds sliced or stray frond fragments at the seam). Seeds 3940777022, 2926455590.
  - birds 0.1%, lightning 0.1%.
  - Never cut or ghosted: double sun, shooting star, lighthouse, sailboat, cabin, volcano smoke.
- Sheet: `08-objects-cut-ghost.png`.
- Fix (the same one also fixes H1 and M2): **keep the main stream in sync**. Wherever the wide pass needs extra randomness for the sides, take it from a side stream (save/restore `rs`). Stars: the piece's `n` stars with `rnd(0,159)`, then side stars on a side stream. Milky way: centre columns on the main stream, side columns on a side stream. Ridges: in WIDE, call `mdisp` etc. anyway (or restore a recorded `rs` per layer). Then every object the piece draws past its edge is drawn identically by the wide pass and continues on the side.

**M2. Trees sliced at the seams.** Forest 35%, snowfield 41%, islands 16% have a piece tree cut at a seam (up to about 45 px). The wide pass's tree loop starts at `GX0 + rnd(0,3)` (`gen_ridges.c` line 239) with a different stream. Barely visible in dense forest, visible on sparse snowfield and island trees. Fix: the centre tree pass as in the piece, side trees from a side stream (M1).

**M3. Glitch strips stop at the seams.** Glitch is skipped in WIDE (`gen_ground.c` line 235), and the piece's row shifts wrap within 160 px (line 241). The glitch bands and inverted block end exactly at the piece edge, so the rectangle of the piece shows in every glitch banner (3% of the collection). Seeds 36, 1774606989, 1248536751. Sheet: `09-glitch.png`. Design call: either keep it (reads as "the piece glitched") or, in the banner, shift the same rows of the sides by the same `sh` (no wrap) so the strips run across the full width. The seam inside the strip then hides in the glitch.

**M4. Fewer stars on the sides at night.** Side star density is a median **0.72x** the centre; **23%** of night banners are under 0.5x (some 0x: 1651082115, 136). Cause: side `n` comes from the diverged stream (lines 91-92) and taller side ridges (C1) cover sky. Fix: KEEP the piece's `n` and give the sides `n * 272 / 160` from a side stream.

**M5. Side ridges are rougher than the piece.** Mean second difference (px/col), piece -> sides: peaks 0.40 -> 0.59, fjord 0.43 -> 0.58, other volcano layers 0.38 -> 0.56, islands far layer 0.19 -> 0.73. The piece smooths peaks (`smooth(prof,1)`) and shapes them with the `p` curve; the side walk does neither. The fjord's 5/4 wall height (line 131) is not applied either. Fixed by H3's "same transform".

### Low

- **L1. Micro seams at the piece edge on ridges.** About half of the tree-less banners differ by more than 8 px in the 8 columns next to a seam. Cause: rim light from `hs[x±2]` clamped to the drawing range (line 215), `smooth()` windows truncated at the array ends (lines 31-39), and snow `jit` near the edge. Usually 1..3 px of a different rim/snow pixel, invisible at 1x, faint at 4x. Fix: in the normal pass these must stay as they are (the piece is fixed), so match them on the side instead: for the first 2..3 side columns, copy the piece's edge column tone pattern or fade the rim.
- **L2. Water.** About 21% have small mismatches below the horizon: glitter (`rnd` around `lx`, lines 130-135) cut when the sun is near an edge, the piece's reflection clamped to columns 0/159 (line 118), and reflections of cut clouds. Clears up with M1/H1.
- **L3. Composition balance.** Shade usage is close on average (centre 0.083/0.249/0.375/0.293 vs sides 0.068/0.239/0.385/0.308; L1 distance > 0.3 in 8.7%). Side structure is a median 0.84 of the centre's, so the centre generally stays the focal area, helped by the sun/moon, water glitter and objects only appearing there. Sides darker in the upper half than the centre by more than 15 points in 11.9% (C1). Sides noticeably emptier (< 0.6 structure) in 4.5% (e.g. 2033914599, 158442765): long flat dark ridges with nothing on them. Optional: side-stream clouds/birds/trees give some life once M1 is in place.

## Per-terrain and per-feature notes

- **Peaks:** good continuation when the far ridge isn't compressed; otherwise C1/C2. Sides a bit too jagged (M5). Sheet `14-peaks.png`.
- **Hills:** the most reliable terrain; roughness matches (0.08 vs 0.11). Overshoot still 38% > 10 px. Sheet `15-hills.png`.
- **Mesas:** H3. Plateaus end at cliffs and sides turn into slopes; obvious at 4x (`19-mesa-4x.png`).
- **Dunes:** H3; sides lose the crests and rise (overshoot mean 12.4 px, the worst terrain for C1).
- **Volcano:** cone, crater glow and smoke are in the piece and never cut (smoke 0%). Other layers inherit C1/C2. Snow bars common with snowfield/snowcaps. Sheet `12-volcano.png`.
- **Islands:** H2; the far peaks layer goes from flat to rough (M5). The horizon highlight and island shorelines continue cleanly. Sheets `03`, `04`.
- **Fjord:** water channel and walls read fine; walls on the sides a bit lower and rougher (M5). Sheet `13-fjord.png`.
- **Ground:** lake/sea reflections continue (KEEP 7). Forest and snowfield edges continue with trees (M2 aside). Meadow tufts and desert texture seamless. Oasis palms see M1.
- **Sky:** sun, haloed sun, low/striped sun, day moon, eclipse, ringed planet, moons: never duplicated or cut (they stay at least 5 px inside the piece). Double sun safe (stream in sync by day). Twin moons M1. Overcast deck seamless.
- **Time:** aurora bands continue seamlessly (`18-aurora.png`); night star density M4; eclipse stars sparse but consistent.
- **Weather:** rain/storm streak density matches across the width; the storm bolt stays in the piece (0.1% cut). Snowfall flakes even. Fog band seamless. Cloud cuts H1 are the weather defect (`07-clouds.png`, `17-storm.png`).
- **Extras:** lighthouse (beam always points into the piece), sailboat, cabin, shooting star: 0 cuts/ghosts. Comet and twin moons M1. Birds fine (0.1%). Milky way band continues (`16-milkyway.png`). Glitch M3.

## Suggested order of work

1. C1 + C2 (bounded, kk-scaled side ridges; snow depth cap). Biggest visible gain, small code in `extend_side`/`draw_layer`.
2. M1 (main stream kept in sync, side streams for side-only randomness). This fixes H1, M2, M4 and the object cuts in one structural change.
3. H2 (island taper) and H3 (terrain transform on the side walk), which also covers M5.
4. Decide on M3 (glitch).
5. Re-run this audit's checks: centre identity, seam-band mismatch per phase, overshoot per layer, object cut/ghost. The centre stays identical by construction (paste), so the goldens are unaffected; the banner is PC/web only.
